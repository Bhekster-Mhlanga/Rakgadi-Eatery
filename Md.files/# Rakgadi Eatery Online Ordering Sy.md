# Rakgadi Eatery Online Ordering System

## Problem Statement

Rakgadi Eatery currently relies on walk\-in ordering, which causes queues, manual order handling, and limited customer convenience.

The business lacks a centralized platform to manage customers, orders, products, notifications, and reporting.

## Proposed Solution

Develop a web\-based online ordering system that allows customers to:

- Create accounts
- Browse menu items
- Place food orders
- Track order progress
- Receive notifications
- Collect food using QR codes and PINs

The system will provide administrators with tools for:

- Managing products
- Managing categories
- Processing orders
- Viewing analytics
- Generating reports

## Goals

- Reduce waiting times
- Improve customer experience
- Improve operational efficiency
- Prevent order collection fraud
- Support future business growth
